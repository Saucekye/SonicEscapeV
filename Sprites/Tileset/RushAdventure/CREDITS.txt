Terrain tiles
-------------
The fill and surface tiles in this folder were cut from the Sonic Rush tileset
sheets supplied by the game's author. Original art: SEGA and Dimps.
If the sheets came from a rip that asks for credit (for example Techokami's
Sonic Rush level rip, or a sheet on The Spriters Resource), credit that ripper
in the game's credits too.

Stage -> tiles (sheet, position, size)
  Night city   neon_tech    green circuit fill + purple hex ledge
  Sunset       brick_ruins  brick-and-stone fill + cream zigzag ledge
  Purple town  roof_town    roof-tile fill + teal-and-red stone cornice (scaled 2x from a half-size sheet)
  Grey city    industrial   crate fill + orange caution girder
  4th floors   teal_tech    teal machinery fill + teal pipe rail
